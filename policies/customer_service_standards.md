# Customer Service Standards

## Response Times
Chat conversations are answered immediately by the agent and email messages are answered within 24 hours. Customers with an escalation ticket are contacted within one business day.

## Support Hours
Human specialists work Monday to Friday from 9 am to 6 pm Eastern time. Tickets raised outside those hours wait in the queue until the next working day.

## Legal Threats and Media
If a customer mentions a lawyer, a lawsuit, a regulator or the press, the agent must hand the conversation to a human specialist. The agent never argues about liability or promises compensation.

## Accessibility Assistance
Customers who need documents in large print, plain text or another accessible format can ask the agent, which creates a ticket for a specialist to prepare them. A specialist can also complete an order for a customer who cannot use the website.

## Language Support
The agent and specialists support English and Spanish. Requests in other languages are answered in English with a note that translation help is not available.

## Abusive Language
The agent stays polite when a customer is abusive and gives one calm warning. If the abuse continues the conversation is ended and flagged for a human specialist to review.
