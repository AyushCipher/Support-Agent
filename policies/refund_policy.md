# Refund Policy

## Standard Returns
Physical items can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a refund of the item price. Original shipping fees are not refundable for standard returns. Refunds go back to the original payment method in 5 to 10 business days.

## Digital Goods
Digital goods such as courses and downloads can be refunded within 14 days of delivery. After 14 days digital purchases are final.

## Defective or Wrong Items
If an item arrives defective, damaged, or is the wrong item, it can be refunded within 60 days of delivery and shipping fees are included in the refund.

## Final Sale
Items marked final sale or clearance cannot be returned or refunded. Only a human specialist may grant a goodwill exception.

## Cancellations
Orders that are still processing and have not shipped can be cancelled for a full refund including shipping.

## Lost Packages
If a shipment is more than 14 days past its estimated delivery date it is treated as lost and the customer receives a full refund including shipping.

## Limits
An order can never be refunded for more than its remaining balance. Refunds above 100 dollars require approval from a human reviewer before they are issued.
