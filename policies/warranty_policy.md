# Warranty Policy

## Warranty Coverage
Electronics and appliances carry a 12 month limited warranty that starts on the delivery date. It covers manufacturing faults that stop a product working as described.

## Warranty Exclusions
The warranty does not cover accidental drops, liquid damage, cosmetic wear, unauthorized repairs or commercial use. Batteries and other consumable parts are covered for 90 days only.

## Filing a Warranty Claim
To open a claim the customer provides the order number, a description of the fault and photos or a short video. A specialist reviews each claim within 3 business days.

## Repair or Replacement
We decide whether to repair or replace the product, and a refund of the purchase price is given only when neither is possible. Repairs take 10 to 15 business days and replacements ship free of charge.

## Extended Protection Plans
A 24 or 36 month protection plan can be added within 30 days of delivery. The plan extends the warranty period and keeps the same coverage and exclusions.

## Warranty Overlap With Other Windows
A warranty claim is separate from the 30 day window for physical items and the 60 day window for faulty arrivals in the refund policy. A product can be outside those windows and still be covered by the warranty.

## Proof of Purchase
The order confirmation email or the order history in the customer's account is accepted as proof of purchase. Gift recipients can use the gift receipt number instead.
