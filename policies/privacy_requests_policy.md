# Privacy Requests Policy

## Data Access Requests
A customer can ask for a copy of the personal data we hold about them. The export is delivered within 30 days as a machine readable file to the email address on the account.

## Data Deletion Requests
A customer can ask us to erase their account and personal data, and this is completed within 30 days. Order and tax records are kept for 7 years because the law requires it, and any active subscription must be cancelled first.

## Identity Verification
Before acting on any privacy request we send a confirmation link to the email address on file. The agent must never act on a privacy request from a person who has not confirmed their identity.

## Marketing Opt-Out
Customers can unsubscribe from promotional email through the link in any message or in their account settings. The change takes effect within 2 business days, and order and shipping notifications are still sent.

## Data Retention
Support chat transcripts are kept for 24 months and then deleted. We never store full card numbers because they are tokenized by the payment processor.

## Cookie Preferences
Visitors can accept, reject or customize analytics and advertising cookies from the banner or the footer link. Rejecting optional cookies does not affect checkout.
