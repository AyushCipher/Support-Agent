# Shipping Policy

## Shipping Methods and Rates
Standard shipping takes 3 to 7 business days and costs 5 dollars 99 cents. Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days and costs 14 dollars 99 cents. Orders with a subtotal over 75 dollars qualify for free standard shipping.

## Processing Time
Orders are packed and handed to the carrier within 1 business day. Orders placed after 2 pm Eastern time are processed the next business day. Nothing is processed on weekends or public holidays.

## Order Tracking
A tracking number is emailed as soon as an order ships. Tracking pages can lag up to 24 hours behind the carrier's own scans, so a brief delay in updates is normal.

## Address Changes
The delivery address can only be changed while an order is still processing. Once an order has shipped, the customer must ask the carrier to redirect the parcel because we cannot edit the label.

## International Shipping
We ship to Canada, the United Kingdom and the European Union, and delivery takes 7 to 21 business days. Customers are responsible for customs duties, import taxes and brokerage fees, and these charges are not reimbursed.

## Failed Delivery Attempts
When nobody is available to accept a parcel the carrier retries up to 3 times, then holds it at a local depot for 5 days. Parcels not collected from the depot are sent back to us and refunded minus the original shipping fee.

## Carrier Delays
Storms, strikes and carrier outages can slow a shipment. A parcel that is late by less than 14 days is not eligible for compensation, and the customer should keep watching the tracking page.

## Delivered but Not Received
If tracking says delivered but the customer has nothing, ask them to check with household members and neighbours and to wait 48 hours. After that a specialist opens a carrier investigation, which takes up to 5 business days.

## Signature Requirements
Orders with a total above 200 dollars require an adult signature on delivery. A carrier will not leave these parcels unattended at a doorstep or in a locker.
