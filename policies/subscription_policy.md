# Subscription Policy

## Cancellation
Customers can cancel a subscription at any time. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period and access continues until then.

## Annual Refunds
Annual plans renewed within the last 14 days are eligible for a full refund. After 14 days annual plans are not refundable, but can still be cancelled at the end of the period.

## Monthly Plans
Monthly plans are not refundable. Cancel before the next renewal date to avoid further charges.
