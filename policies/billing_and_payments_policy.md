# Billing and Payments Policy

## Accepted Payment Methods
We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal, Apple Pay and our own gift cards. We do not accept personal checks, cash on delivery or cryptocurrency.

## Gift Cards and Store Credit
Gift cards never expire and cannot be exchanged for cash. Store credit granted as a goodwill gesture is valid for 12 months and cannot be used to buy gift cards.

## Promo Codes
Only one promo code can be used per order and codes cannot be combined with each other. A code cannot be added after the order has been placed and expired codes are not honored.

## Price Adjustments
If the price of an item drops on our site within 14 days of purchase, the customer can request a one time adjustment paid as store credit. Final sale and clearance items are not eligible.

## Sales Tax
Sales tax is calculated from the delivery address at checkout. Tax is returned together with the price whenever an item is refunded.

## Failed Subscription Payments
When a subscription renewal payment fails we retry 3 times over 7 days. If every retry fails the subscription is suspended and access ends until the customer updates their payment method in account settings.

## Invoices and Receipts
Invoices are available at any time in the order history of the account. Customers in the European Union can request a VAT invoice with their company details.
