# Fraud and Chargebacks Policy

## Chargebacks
When a customer disputes a charge with their bank we cannot also refund the same charge. We send evidence to the bank within 7 days and the customer should be advised to contact us before disputing. A refund and a chargeback are never both paid for one order.

## Suspected Fraudulent Orders
Orders flagged by the risk system are held for up to 48 hours for manual review and the buyer may be asked to verify their details. Orders that fail review are cancelled and the money is returned to the original payment method.

## Unauthorized Charges
When a customer reports a charge they did not make, the agent must not issue a refund itself. It creates an urgent ticket for the fraud team and tells the customer that they will hear back within one business day.

## Account Takeover
Signs of a hijacked account include an unexpected password change or a new delivery address the customer does not recognize. The agent locks the account, sends a password reset link to the verified email and escalates to the security team.

## Duplicate Charges
If a customer was billed twice for the same order, the extra charge is returned in full once billing confirms it. Pending authorizations that are not captured disappear from the statement in 3 to 5 days.
